# Nexora focused offers

Prepared 10 October 2026. Proposal preparation material; not a production-readiness certificate. Choose one offer for the buyer's requirement.

## Website/CMS

For teams that need to maintain public company, service and information pages. Nexora supports branded content, navigation and page draft/preview/publication. Evaluate with three agreed sample pages and one staff editor: change a draft, preview it, publish, reload the public page, then unpublish. A website can be scoped separately from paid membership.

Evidence: [CMS delivery record](../stages/STAGE-16-CMS-PUBLISHING.md) and [public website screenshot](pitch/screens/website.png). This is historical product evidence; a current walkthrough and deployment acceptance are still required. Do not promise ecommerce, booking, a custom quotation form, multilingual content or external CRM integration without verifying that scope. Hosting/domain/TLS and live media scanning require operational agreement.

## Signed-in portal

For supported profile self-service and controlled private information access. Evaluate with synthetic contacts and explicit access grants, one private information page and the relevant profile journey. Verify both authorised access and denial/revocation. Portal subscriptions are optional to the proposed commercial scope; underlying tenant/contact/identity dependencies remain.

Evidence: [portal configuration delivery](../stages/STAGE-19-PORTAL-CONFIGURATION.md), [product targeting source review](middle-east/PRODUCT-TARGETING.md) and [portal screenshot](pitch/screens/portal.png). Custom pages are bounded plain text; arbitrary application forms, document workflows, booking, checkout and third-party CRM synchronisation are separate requirements. Production identity must be accepted before live user onboarding.

## Company/contact CRM

For verified first-system adoption or a specifically agreed supported use case. Demonstrate one company and its contacts, the relevant profile and record workflow. Confirm imports/exports, permissions and the customer's required fields during discovery. Existing-system replacement/integration is not an automatic deliverable. The AI assistant is optional and provider/authentication/cost dependent; automatic record access/actions are not assumed.

## Membership workflow

For organisations with a verified suitable adoption need. Demonstrate one membership product, applicant, staff review and member outcome using synthetic/anonymised data. Agree acceptance, access isolation and data reconciliation. The existing [90-second narrated demo](pitch/nexora-product-demo-male.mp4) demonstrates the core membership platform; it is not a dedicated CMS/portal or AI-chat demo.

## Common proposal terms

Agree the problem, sample journey, acceptance results, currency/price, responsibilities, milestones, support and exit/export scope in writing. Regional deployments, Arabic localisation, payments/tax and uptime claims require separate evidence. Nexora has no verified customer testimonial or measured savings in this campaign; do not invent either. Final scope and production use depend on current demonstration and operational acceptance.
