How do we start?

Email Nexora to arrange a focused demonstration. We’ll discuss your membership process and agree whether a scoped assisted evaluation is a useful next step.

Can the client choose the deployment URL?

The deployment URL is agreed with each client as part of the hosting and production setup. A planned URL does not mean an environment is already deployed.

What is included in an evaluation?

Scope is agreed around one association, one membership product and the relevant staff and member journey, using demonstration or anonymised data with acceptance criteria.

What does Nexora cost?

Contact Nexora for evaluation and deployment pricing. Configuration, migration, hosting, support and any external services are agreed for the actual scope.
