# Starting founder sales

## The first conversation

You do not need to perform as a salesperson. Your job is to find a recurring problem, understand its cost, and show one relevant working journey. Open with:

> I am the founder of Nexora. We've built a membership management platform for associations that connects company and contact records, membership applications, staff review and member self-service. I would like to understand your current process and show you the relevant parts of the working product.

Treat existing software respectfully. Many prospects already have portals and membership systems. An old-looking website is not evidence that their administration is broken.

## Four-week plan

| Period | Actions | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Watch the demo, practise a 10-minute walkthrough, choose five high-fit prospects, verify legal entity and contact route, prepare business email and a truthful product page | Five researched organisations and personalised opening lines |
| Week 2 | Send five individually written messages, add five researched prospects, follow up once after 5–7 working days | Sent date, reply, next step, opt-out; avoid judging success from opens |
| Week 3 | Run discovery calls; demonstrate only the workflow they describe; ask for an anonymised example of their application process | Repeated problems, current systems, budget range, decision process |
| Week 4 | Offer one scoped pilot; document acceptance criteria and support limits; revise positioning based on feedback | A pilot decision or a clear reason the fit failed |

Targets are activity goals, not a forecast: 15–20 carefully selected organisations, three qualified conversations and one pilot proposal. Small samples cannot establish a dependable conversion rate. If nobody replies after 15–20 relevant messages, review the audience and wording before increasing volume.

## First email

Subject: A short Nexora demo for [association]'s membership team

Hi [name or membership team],

I saw that [one specific fact from their official membership page].

We've built Nexora, a membership management platform for associations. It brings company and contact records, membership applications, staff review and member self-service into one connected system, with the aim of reducing repeated updates and making each member's information easier to follow.

Nexora also supports an AI assistant inside the CRM workspace. When enabled, your team can access AI assistance alongside their day-to-day membership work.

I've attached a 90-second narrated demonstration of Nexora. It also has on-screen captions, so you can watch it with or without sound.

We're now inviting a small number of associations to explore a focused evaluation around their own membership process. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation to see whether Nexora could be useful for [association]?

Best,
Ramish
Founder, Nexora
[actual business name, website and reply address]

If this is not relevant, reply “no thanks” and I will not follow up.

Ask a general inbox to route this to the person responsible for membership operations. Use the downloadable MP4 attachment in the first email; keep pricing and detailed implementation scope for the conversation. See [demo sharing](DEMO-SHARING.md). “We've built” describes the working product; it does not imply completed production onboarding, active external providers or an established customer base.

The AI claim is limited to a configurable embedded assistant workspace. Confirm the upstream service, user authentication and agreed provider costs before offering it. Do not claim it automatically reads or changes CRM records, shares Nexora sign-in, or executes workflows; those capabilities are separate, and the existing CRM data bridge is disabled. The current tour does not demonstrate AI. Explain the actual integration and limits on the call before agreeing any AI scope.

## Follow-up

After 5–7 working days:

> Hi [name], a brief follow-up on membership administration. Would it be useful to compare your current application and company-contact process with a short Nexora demo? If this is not a priority, I am happy to leave it here.

One final follow-up after a further 7–10 working days is enough. Stop immediately on an objection. ## Discovery call: 20 minutes

1. First 2 minutes: explain that you want to understand their process and agree whether a demo is worthwhile.
2. Next 10 minutes: ask them to describe the last application or renewal. Where did staff re-enter data? What went wrong? How many times each month? What systems are involved? Who changes company contacts? What must members do themselves?
3. Next 5 minutes: show one relevant journey. Ask whether this would improve their process and what is missing.
4. Last 3 minutes: identify decision-maker, current contract renewal date, likely budget and a concrete next step.

Record the buyer's words, not just “interested”. Qualification requires a real problem, a responsible owner, a plausible budget, an acceptable migration/integration scope and a decision window. A friendly demo with no next step is not a qualified opportunity.

## Objections

| Buyer says | Useful response |
| --- | --- |
| We already have a CRM | Which membership process still takes work outside it? If none, a change may not be worthwhile. |
| We cannot risk a migration | Start with synthetic or anonymised records and one workflow; validate an export and reconciliation before any cutover. |
| You are a new supplier | That is fair. Offer a scoped pilot with written acceptance criteria and an exit/export plan. Do not imply established customers or uptime history. |
| The price is high | Compare it with their measured work and current spend. Reduce scope before reducing price. |
| We need [missing feature] | State its actual status. Discuss separately scoped development only if it supports the product direction. |

## Pilot proposal

Offer a 30-day assisted evaluation using synthetic/anonymised data. Agree: one configured membership product, one staff reviewer, one member journey, an import rehearsal, an export, and a weekly feedback call. Price at **£500**, credited toward the onboarding fee if they proceed within 60 days. Do not charge the full annual subscription until the agreed hosted production scope is ready and accepted. A pilot is not permission to run live payments or upload sensitive documents to an unverified deployment.

Success must be measurable: the applicant can submit and reload a receipt, staff can review and activate the membership, the correct member can see the result, unauthorised users cannot see it, and records export/reconcile. Measure the time taken against their existing process; do not promise a percentage saving beforehand.

## UK outreach basics

Check that the recipient organisation is incorporated before relying on corporate B2B email rules. Under current ICO guidance, PECR generally does not require consent for corporate-subscriber marketing emails, but identity and an opt-out address are required; UK GDPR still applies to named business contacts. Sole traders, some partnerships and unincorporated bodies have different rules. Treat uncertain status as an individual subscriber. Use a relevant public business route, record the source, explain personal-data use where applicable, honour objections, and skip tracking pixels. See [ICO B2B marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/), reviewed 7 October 2026; ICO notes this guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act.

Prepare a credible business domain/mailbox, real contact details, privacy notice, and email authentication before outreach. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC with your chosen provider. Ask for references/testimonials only after delivery, with the customer's consent.
