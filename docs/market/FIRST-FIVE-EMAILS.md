# Five first-pitch emails

Revised 8 October 2026. Drafts only; nothing has been sent. These messages introduce the product we have built, connect it to the association and offer a short demonstration with one clear next step. Contact sources and fit assumptions are in [the prospect list](UK-PROSPECTS.md). Verify the legal entity and current address before outreach and record opt-outs.

Attach [nexora-product-demo-male.mp4](pitch/nexora-product-demo-male.mp4) and replace the signature placeholders before sending. The recipient receives the video file and can download it; no public hosting or viewing link is needed. See [demo sharing](DEMO-SHARING.md). Use a single MP4 attachment and leave pricing and detailed module lists for the conversation. “We've built” describes the working product, not completed production onboarding or an established customer base.

AI positioning: Nexora supports an embedded NexOrchestr AI workspace when the upstream assistant is configured and available. This is access to AI within the CRM; automatic access to CRM records, shared sign-in, record changes and AI-driven automations are not established by embedding. The CRM data bridge remains disabled. Verify the configured assistant and its authentication/provider arrangement before including it in a live demonstration or contracted scope. The current 90-second tour shows core membership features; the narration mentions configurable AI access but does not show an AI chat. Describe the benefit of access within the workspace rather than claiming a unique AI capability or performance advantage.

GTMA brings together a network of manufacturing businesses, each with its own people and membership information to manage.
