## Assessment and changes

The campaign already has personalised official-source research, a real product video, duplicate checks, opt-outs and a reply monitor. The major weakness is that a website announcement or membership model has been treated as a promising research signal without establishing an available buyer, current requirement or usable contact route. Repeated hourly searches of the same held companies do not move them towards a purchase.

| Gap | Implemented response | Evidence needed before progressing |
|---|---|---|
| Broad product pitch | Lead with one offer: website/CMS, supported portal, CRM or membership | One relevant workflow and capability match |
| Unknown buyer need | Separate observed facts, problem hypotheses and buyer-confirmed problems | Current requirement, supplier invitation or direct confirmation |
| General inboxes | Record buyer role and contact-purpose limits | Relevant decision-maker or an appropriate routing channel |
| Research without prioritisation | Score need, capability fit, buyer access and timing separately from hard sending gates | Source links and dates for each score |
| Video mismatch | Use the offer brief and relevant walkthrough; accurately describe the existing membership video | Website/portal demonstration before an offer-specific proposal |
| No progression record | Track research, ready, sent, replied, discovery, qualified, proposal, pilot and won/lost | A dated next action, owner and agreed decision point |
| Limited trust evidence | Prepare narrow acceptance criteria, support limits and an exit/export plan | Real demonstration and operational dependencies; no invented testimonials |
| Cold email as the only path | Prioritise requested proposals and warm introductions, then appropriate supplier channels | Permission or verified lawful route for the actual channel |

These are implementation choices for Nexora, not a guaranteed conversion formula. [YC's founder-sales guidance](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/enterprise-sales-for-hackers) supports problem discovery, qualifying buyers and carrying opportunities through a sales process. Our ranking is a research priority, never a purchase probability.

Score each dimension 0–3. Need: unknown (0), observed workflow/project signal (1), explicit current buyer requirement (2), confirmed recurring problem with a measured baseline (3). Capability fit: unknown (0), partial match (1), bounded supported journey (2), demonstrated acceptance match (3). Buyer access: unknown/general customer enquiries (0), verified relevant role channel (1), named responsible buyer (2), engaged decision-maker/champion (3). Timing: unknown (0), dated project signal (1), verified active decision window (2), buyer-agreed decision date (3). Record the evidence behind every score; clearance is assessed separately. A high score cannot override an opt-out, missing lawful route or unsupported must-have.

## Qualification and discovery worksheet

Record the buyer's words and source/date for each answer:

- The last real example of the problem; frequency, people involved and measured time/cost.
- Current software, contract dates, existing provider and what should remain.
- Must-have functions, integrations, content/data volumes, languages and access roles.
- Sponsor, decision-maker, procurement steps, budget range, currency and decision window.
- One relevant journey to demonstrate and the buyer's acceptance criteria.
- Hosting, identity, privacy, security, backup, support and data export requirements.
- A specific agreed next action, owner and date; otherwise keep the opportunity unqualified.

Do not promise a saving until there is a baseline. Estimate value from measured minutes per task multiplied by frequency and the buyer's own cost assumptions. Keep assumptions visible. Do not use percentages from invented evidence.

## Proposal and pilot

Website evaluation: agreed sample pages, one editor, draft/preview/publish/unpublish and public reload. Portal evaluation: an explicitly granted test contact, one permitted private page and profile journey, and blocked access for an unauthorised identity. CRM/membership evaluation: one company/contact and one application/review journey, with an export/reconciliation if required and supported. Use synthetic/anonymised data until the deployment and data-handling scope is accepted.

Each proposal states deliverables and exclusions, milestones, responsibilities, currency, payment terms, support hours, change-control, acceptance method and exit/export arrangements. Pricing and dates must be agreed from actual scope. Arabic localisation, regional hosting, payments/tax, production identity, arbitrary forms, booking/ecommerce, automated migrations and external integrations are not assumed available. See [offer briefs](OFFER-BRIEFS.md).

Follow-ups are prepared only: one useful follow-up after 5–7 business days and an optional close-out after a further 7–10, using the recipient's calendar. Current automation excludes follow-up sending; do not enable it implicitly. An objection stops outreach immediately. A paid pilot, accepted results and a subsequent production decision are distinct stages; obtain permission before publishing any reference or testimonial.

## Measurement and learning

Use `python scripts/Review-MarketingCampaign.py` to generate the current priority queue, revised held drafts and pipeline baseline. It reads records and prepares local files; it cannot send email. Keep subsequent buyer evidence and stage changes in `docs/market/acquisition/PIPELINE.json`, preserving history.

Review weekly: unique researched organisations; need-qualified and send-eligible counts; held reasons; confirmed unique sends; human positive replies; discovery calls held; qualified opportunities; proposals; accepted pilots; paid clients. Every rate states its denominator and cohort/date. Gmail acceptance is not delivery. Automatic replies and declines are not positive conversions. Open tracking is not used.

If 15–20 eligible, relevant messages have no positive human replies after a reasonable response window, review the segment, buyer, offer and proof before expanding volume. Current historical mail is only 1–2 days old, so its lack of positive replies cannot establish that a pitch has failed.

## Sending basis is separate from buyer interest

An open RFP is useful evidence of demand and a defined submission route; it is not the only possible lawful B2B contact route. Cold proposals can be lawful in some countries and recipient categories. Do not assert a universal Middle East ban or universal B2B exemption. Verify the actual jurisdiction, contact category, use of personal data and channel. The campaign's existing conservative gate permits documented consent **or a verified applicable lawful route**. A published enquiries address alone establishes neither buying intent nor clearance.
