# Nexora US association shortlist — 8 October 2026

118 candidate organisations were screened; 50 are shortlisted below and 30 selected for the first outreach. Rank expresses research priority based on the company/contact membership model, visible application or account journey, and availability of a published association contact. It is not a purchase probability or evidence of budget, dissatisfaction, organisation size, or active procurement.

The first 30 have verified public business email routes and a source-linked observation for the pitch. General association mailboxes may need to route the email internally. Existing portals are visible at many prospects; switching interest and integrations are unknown. The 20 reserves need more contact or scope qualification before sending.

## Discovery before quoting or agreeing a production scope

Ask who manages membership operations, how companies and employees are connected, where application review happens, which existing systems must connect, and whether they are evaluating software. Confirm hosting, security, data import, US payment and tax requirements before agreeing delivery. Start with one workflow and a short demonstration. The demo uses existing sample data; no US customer deployment, quantified savings or autonomous AI actions are claimed.

All contacts were taken from public association pages or association publications. No member directory addresses, guessed personal emails or purchased lists were used. Source captures are retained in this folder; pages and staff roles can change.
