# First CRM adoption — US prospect qualification

10 October scope note: these first-system evidence rules apply to CRM/membership-system adoption. The current [dual-market policy](../DUAL-MARKET-CAMPAIGN.md) also allows separately relevant website/CMS and supported portal offers; existing/unknown CRM alone does not exclude those. Its five-hour cadence replaces historical hourly wording below.

Updated 9 October 2026 at the user's request. Prioritise membership organisations that could adopt their first dedicated CRM or membership system. Nexora is a membership product; a manufacturer with only a general sales pipeline is not automatically a suitable buyer. Search local/regional trade associations, professional bodies and business membership organisations with recurring dues, member records and an administrator who can evaluate software. Size alone does not establish software absence, budget or fit.

## Evidence and sending decisions

Read and update [CRM-QUALIFICATION.json](research/CRM-QUALIFICATION.json) when screening candidates. Existing exclusions and research-only findings are not send-ready leads; reclassify only with new verified evidence, keeping dated history. The [initial check](research/CRM-QUALIFICATION.md) includes confirmed existing-system exclusions and unresolved reserve candidates. Preserve all unknowns as unknown.

| crmStatus | Required evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| confirmed_no_crm | The organisation explicitly confirms no dedicated CRM or membership system in a current official source or direct conversation | Eligible after all normal contact, fit, reply and duplicate checks |
| manual_records_evidence | Current official material explicitly describes member records, dues/renewal tracking or administrative handover in spreadsheets or manual files, with no contradictory existing-system evidence | Eligible for one qualification pitch; absence of CRM is still unconfirmed |
| existing_system | Verified current CRM/association-management vendor use, an official vendor-hosted membership portal, or explicit existing membership software | Exclude from this campaign |
| unknown_system | No reliable current information, only a simple site, a PDF application, an email contact route or a missing login | Research only; do not send |

Record organisation, US presence, membership model, administrator contact/role, crmStatus, source URL, checkedAt, source date, manualWorkflowEvidence, existingSystemEvidence, confidence and outreachDecision. Do not label unknown as no CRM. An official spreadsheet process can coexist with a CRM; use the qualification question rather than assert software absence. A generic member login is a clue to investigate, not by itself proof of CRM use. A site running WordPress is neither evidence for nor against CRM. A vendor form widget alone does not establish the system used for membership records.

Check each homepage, public membership/join/renewal pages and official staff/operations material. Inspect visible links and publicly supplied HTML for identifiable membership vendors such as GrowthZone, MemberClicks, WildApricot, iMIS, YourMembership and MemberSuite; verify context before excluding. Never sign in, inspect private records, scrape member contacts or bypass access restrictions. Search the organisation name plus CRM, membership software, member portal, spreadsheet and renewal tracking. Prior vendor customer stories and outdated policies require a current check. A statement about spreadsheets before a migration is not current evidence of no CRM.

Prefer a live current workflow or an official document updated within 24 months. Older documents are research leads, not send eligibility unless current operations are corroborated. Search-engine crawl/publication timestamps do not establish when an association adopted or abandoned software.

## Search patterns

- US trade association "membership records" "spreadsheet"
- Association "membership secretary" "Excel" "renewals"
- Association "membership coordinator" "spreadsheet" "member records"
- Regional trade association "membership administration" "manual"
- [association name] "membership software" or "CRM" or "member portal"

Search combinations are leads, not proof. Exclude template vendors, software marketing pages and non-US organisations. A small hobby club may have manual processes but still be a weak commercial fit; prioritise an organisation with paid memberships and a realistic workflow to evaluate. Do not invent a budget.

## Pitch adaptation

Use one precise observation from the verified manual process, describe the built Nexora product, include the optional AI assistant and narrated video, then ask one question. For manual_records_evidence use:

> Are you currently managing membership records in spreadsheets, or do you already use a dedicated CRM or membership system?

Do not assert they have no CRM, their current process is failing, or a migration is already supported and verified. For confirmed_no_crm, ask whether a focused look at the documented workflow would help. Keep the existing sender, postal address, ad identification and reply-based opt-out. If a reply confirms an existing system, record existing_system and stop this prospect's outreach; honour any broader refusal. If it expresses interest, apply the campaign's positive-response stop and notify the user.

The existing hourly schedule remains one fresh organisation per hour and at most 24 per UK date. Skip sending when no candidate meets this stricter filter. Previously sent organisations remain excluded; their old messages and verification are historical evidence.
