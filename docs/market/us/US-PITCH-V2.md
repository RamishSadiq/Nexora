## Message structure

The latest user instruction prioritises first CRM adoption. Apply [CRM-FIRST-TARGETING.md](CRM-FIRST-TARGETING.md) before outreach. For a prospect with credible current manual membership administration but unconfirmed CRM absence, replace the closing question with: "Are you currently managing membership records in spreadsheets, or do you already use a dedicated CRM or membership system?" Use one question. Verified existing-system prospects are excluded, and unknown-system prospects remain research-only. Preserve the earlier examples below as historical copy rather than proof that those prospects meet the new filter.

Keep the main copy around 90–130 words, excluding signature and required footer. Use one verified fact about the prospect and one relevant workflow. Write directly and naturally. Do not imply its current system is failing. Give a clear reason to look at the built product and end with one easy question. Use a concise, truthful subject such as "Nexora demo for [association]'s membership team" or "Nexora and [association]'s company memberships". Do not use false Re:/Fwd:, invented scarcity, exaggerated AI claims or promised savings.

Suggested main copy:

Hi [name or membership team],

I noticed [verified fact about company membership, employee access or application review]. That is the kind of [specific workflow] we built Nexora to support.

Nexora connects member-company records, their contacts, membership applications, staff review and member self-service. [Explain the relevant capability in one concrete sentence only if it adds something specific.]

An optional AI assistant can also be enabled inside the CRM, so staff can use AI assistance alongside their membership work.
