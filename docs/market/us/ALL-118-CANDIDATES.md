Not all 118 are fully qualified: the first 30 have public contact and company-membership evidence; the remaining candidates are reserves or deferred research.

Fit is inferred from membership structure, not confirmed need, budget or willingness to buy. Deferring a candidate does not mean Nexora could never serve it. Candidate IDs are stable research IDs and are separate from shortlist priority ranks.
