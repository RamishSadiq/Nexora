# Client search improvements

Added 10 October; scope updated 12 October 2026 at the user's request. Apply to Australia, United States, New Zealand and Canada under [the current campaign policy](MULTI-MARKET-CAMPAIGN.md): one new prospect per country, four total per rolling five hours, with the shared daily maximum of 24.

## 1. Search by client segment and offer

Keep separate research cohorts for each market and offer. Match one observed workflow to one supported module using [the offer briefs](OFFER-BRIEFS.md).

| Segment | Initial offer | Responsible role to research | Example search starting points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Associations and membership organisations | Membership/application administration | Membership manager, operations lead, executive director | `[country] [industry] association membership application`; `[association] membership administrator vacancy` |
| Private service businesses, studios, consultancies and equipment suppliers | Website/CMS | Owner, marketing lead, content/operations manager | `[city] [sector] new branch`; `[business] new services website`; `[business] website supplier request` |
| Organisations with customer/member information and access workflows | Supported portal | Operations lead, customer/member services lead | `[organisation] member portal`; `[organisation] customer information access`; `[organisation] portal tender` |
| Businesses adopting their first company/contact or membership system | CRM or membership | Owner, operations lead, membership manager | `[sector] [country] CRM procurement`; `[organisation] contact administration vacancy` |

These queries are discovery starting points, not evidence of demand. Confirm facts on official sources, current dates and local presence. Apply the existing first-system CRM/membership criteria; an existing CRM does not disqualify a separate website or portal opportunity. Portal access requirements must match supported functions.

## 2. Prioritise current buying or change signals

Look for new branches, expansion, service launches, dated supplier requests, current website/content projects and explicit administrative problems. Operations hiring is a change signal only unless the vacancy actually describes a relevant workflow. Record the source URL, source/check dates and exact observed fact. Check that tenders are open, within capability and not already awarded.

Separate `observedFact`, `problemHypothesis` and `buyerConfirmedProblem`. Keep buying interest, budget and timing unknown until evidenced. A dated or coming-soon website alone does not prove dissatisfaction, a vacant provider or an active buying decision. Prefer stronger current evidence when available; it improves ranking without becoming a universal invitation requirement for a cold first pitch.

## 3. Research the responsible buyer

Find the role that owns the specific workflow, then verify its current occupant and published relevant business contact where available. Record role, name, source/date and contact purpose. A relevant general business inbox can route a pitch; record that buyer access remains unconfirmed. Do not guess addresses or harvest member directories. Preserve source and contact restrictions from the current operating policy.

## 4. Write one outcome-focused pitch

Use one verified observation, one offer, one concrete supported outcome and one question. Examples: staff can maintain service pages through draft/preview/publication; administrators can connect company/contact records; an explicitly authorised member can access a permitted private page. Do not turn a hypothesis into a claim about the prospect's problems.

Use a relevant 15-minute demonstration or small evaluation as the next step. Follow the active pitch templates and sender/attachment requirements. Track copy version with market, segment and offer so results can be compared. Avoid changing several variables within a cohort without recording the change.

## 5. Build proof and trust that match the offer

| Asset | Current evidence/status | Next deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Membership introduction | Existing narrated product video; still the required campaign attachment | Keep its description accurate and confirm the walkthrough against current functionality |
| Website/CMS demonstration | Dedicated shareable demonstration outstanding | Show an editor managing agreed sample pages through draft, preview and publication |
| Portal demonstration | Dedicated shareable demonstration outstanding | Show authorised private-page/profile access and blocked unauthorised access |
| Public product page | Publishing outstanding; offer briefs are local preparation | Explain module outcomes, scope, real founder/contact details and a clear demonstration request route |
| Customer references | No verified customer proof recorded | Seek publication permission only after an actual accepted project |

Record the proof asset used and any mismatch in each opportunity. Do not imply that these outstanding assets are complete or published. Do not invent testimonials, savings, production readiness or regional capabilities. Keep the existing attachment until the campaign is explicitly updated to a verified replacement.

## 6. Develop referrals and partnerships

Maintain a separate research/preparation queue for founder-provided relationships, relevant web/design agencies and industry consultants. Record market, sector, supported complementary offer, public evidence, relationship source, next action and owner. Use [the introduction request](acquisition/INTRODUCTION-REQUEST.md) to prepare a specific introduction request for an actual relationship.

No known founder relationship or recipient willingness should be invented. This addition enables research and preparation; it does not enable automatic partner messages, social messages, calls, contact-form submissions or paid campaigns. Referral candidates are not counted as sales opportunities until there is a relevant buyer introduction.

## 7. Measure progression and propose a scoped first project

Record opportunities in `acquisition/PIPELINE.json` with market, segment, offer, acquisition channel, signal/source/date, buyer role/contact evidence, pitch version, proof asset/gap, stage, next action, owner and due date. Preserve existing messages, buyer evidence and history. Missing facts remain unknown; they must not be silently filled from assumptions.

Review once per week, using a recorded review date to avoid duplicating the review every five-hour run. Compare unique sends, positive human replies, discovery calls held, qualified opportunities, proposals, accepted pilots and paying clients by market/segment/offer/channel and pitch version. State the cohort, dates, numerator and denominator; unavailable observations remain unknown. Gmail acceptance is sending only. Sent counts alone do not establish campaign success.

After 15–20 relevant eligible messages in a cohort and at least 7 business days of response time, a lack of positive replies triggers a review of targeting, buyer, offer and proof before increasing volume. This is an initial operating heuristic, not a claimed benchmark or guarantee. Do not conclude that freshly sent pitches have failed.

On buyer interest, follow the existing positive-response stop and present the next action to the founder. Use [the discovery/proposal worksheet](acquisition/DISCOVERY-PROPOSAL.md) to establish workflow, current systems, decision-maker, budget/currency, timing and dependencies. Propose a small first project with explicit deliverables, acceptance, responsibilities, support and exit/export terms. No automatic replies, quotes, meetings or follow-ups are enabled by this addition.

## Every-run checklist

1. Research all four countries in separate segment/offer cohorts; prefer fresh current change signals.
2. Record factual sources, buyer access and one supported outcome; keep unknowns explicit.
3. Rank candidates using existing need/fit/buyer/timing scores and current sending checks.
4. Prepare/send only under the current operating policy, and record copy/proof/next action.
5. Update pipeline progression; perform a weekly cohort review when due. Keep proof and referral gaps visible in the run report when actionable.

Immediate priorities: stronger current signals and buyer contacts during research, then dedicated website/portal demonstrations and a truthful public product page. The seven improvements are operating instructions; outstanding trust assets and real referral relationships remain deliverables.
