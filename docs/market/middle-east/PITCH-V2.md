# Focused first-pitch templates

10 October 2026. Replaces general product wording for new outreach only. Preserve existing sent messages and held-source history. All placeholders must be resolved from evidence; country/contact clearance and all current sending controls still apply.

Keep the main message around 90–130 words, excluding the required footer. Lead with one verified fact, one relevant supported outcome, a small evaluation and one question. Do not suggest a missing or broken process from a coming-soon website. Unknown problems stay hypotheses. Do not claim a provider has not already been appointed.
