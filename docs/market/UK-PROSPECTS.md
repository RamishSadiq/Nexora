## Referral channels

[Trade Association Forum](https://www.taforum.org/) publishes a member directory and secretariat@taforum.org. [British Cleaning Council](https://britishcleaningcouncil.org/) publishes info@britishcleaningcouncil.org and links to constituent associations. Treat these as networking/referral routes, not assumed software buyers. A supplier membership or sponsorship costs money and should follow evidence that the audience is useful.

## Before emailing

For each row, verify the current legal entity on Companies House or the appropriate register, screen against opt-outs, confirm the contact route still applies, and personalise one question from its website. Incorporation is not implied merely by having a business-domain email. BPMA's [Companies House record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/04546290) confirms a company limited by guarantee; BAFSA's contact-page footer provides a registered company number. The other entities still require that pre-send check. See the [ICO source and outreach notes](SALES-PLAYBOOK.md#uk-outreach-basics).

Track: organisation, source URL, verified date, legal entity/status, contact role, problem hypothesis, stage, first-contact date, follow-up date, objection/opt-out, current software, renewal date, budget, decision-maker and next agreed action. Keep actual buying pain distinct from the hypothesis. The [8 October outreach ledger](outreach/2026-10-08/STATUS.md) records all eleven eligible recipients confirmed in Gmail Sent, each with its video attachment verified, and BASA held without prior permission. Google quota backoff guidance was applied to complete the remaining sends. Do not resend confirmed messages.
