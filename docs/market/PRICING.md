# Proposed UK pricing

Recommendation as of 7 October 2026: quote **£2,500 onboarding + £4,800/year** for the normal first commercial package, with a limited founding offer of **£1,500 + £3,000/year** for up to three narrowly scoped pilots converting to production. Prices exclude VAT if applicable. These are proposed selling prices, not observed willingness to pay or quotes for infrastructure.

“Onboarding” means configuration, an agreed migration and training. It is not a lifetime software purchase or ownership of the source. The annual fee pays for hosted use, updates and bounded support. Production subscriptions depend on completing the agreed identity, hosting, backup and provider acceptance work.

## Packaging

| Offer | One-time onboarding | Annual subscription | Year-one total | Proposed scope |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Assisted evaluation | £500 | None | £500 | 30 days; synthetic/anonymised data; one workflow; credited against onboarding if proceeding within 60 days |
| Founding partner | £1,500 | £3,000 | £4,500 | First three customers; one association; three staff; up to 1,000 active memberships/5,000 CRM contacts; 10 GB; standard CRM/membership/portal and basic website |
| Standard | £2,500 | £4,800 | £7,300 | One association; five staff; up to 2,500 active memberships/10,000 contacts; 20 GB; same core scope; agreed configuration |
| Growth | From £4,500 | From £7,800 | From £12,300 | Up to ten staff; more records/workflows; migration and load assessment before a firm quote |

Record/storage allowances are proposed commercial boundaries, not proven throughput, load results or implemented billing enforcement. Confirm both technical capacity and enforcement before promising them. A founding subscription price is fixed for its first 12 months; specify the renewal price in the contract upfront, avoiding an unexpected jump. £3,600/year is a reasonable proposed year-two founding renewal, subject to measured support cost and buyer acceptance. Standard renewal stays £4,800 unless an agreed price-change clause applies.

Include one membership-product configuration, one agreed CSV import of contacts/organisations with sample validation, two training sessions and business-hours email support. Give the customer a CSV export and an exit process. Start subscription billing on accepted production availability, annually in advance. Split onboarding 50% at agreed kickoff and 50% on acceptance. Define acceptance criteria, change control and any cancellation/refund terms in the actual contract.

Do not bundle bespoke integrations, unlimited support, custom website design, historical document cleanup, a full accounting replacement, live email/SMS, paid checkout or enterprise uptime guarantees into this price. Existing events, finance and workflow screens can be evaluated separately; source implementation does not establish every customer's operational acceptance. Provider fees and additional services require separate scoped quotes.

## Cost floor: replace assumptions with real quotes

Illustrative annual allocation **per Standard customer** with three paying customers sharing operating infrastructure:

| Cost | Assumption | Annual allocation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Hosting, database, backups, monitoring | £250/month total shared operating budget / three customers; placeholder, not vendor quote | £1,000 |
| Support | 2 hours/month × £35 fully loaded hourly cost | £840 |
| Maintenance/security/release allocation | 20 hours/year × £35 | £700 |
| Delivery contingency | 15% of the above £2,540 | £381 |
| Total recurring delivery cost | Before sales, tax, payment fees and original development investment | **£2,921** |
| Contribution at £4,800/year | £4,800 − £2,921 | **£1,879 (39.1%)** |

With only one customer bearing the entire £3,000 annual infrastructure budget, this model becomes (£3,000 + £840 + £700) × 1.15 = **£5,221/year**. The £4,800 package then loses £421 before sales and tax. Budget the early shared platform investment separately; a £3,000 founding subscription is not automatically sustainable. Dedicated hosting, licensed SQL Server, high file/email volume or intensive support may raise costs significantly.

Onboarding example: 30 hours × £35 = £1,050 labour, plus £200 import/tooling allocation and 20% contingency = **£1,500** cost. At £2,500, contribution is £1,000 (40%). Founding onboarding at £1,500 breaks even under this assumption; if it needs 60 hours, rescope or requote before work. Restrict early migration scope and measure actual time.

At 39.1% contribution, Standard is deliberately conservative rather than a mature high-margin SaaS price. Longer term, target lower support cost through repeatable onboarding and self-service, or increase the annual price. Record actual infrastructure, hours, churn and acquisition cost after each pilot; reprice after the first three. Do not base discounts on assumed volume.

## A practical quote sentence

> For the agreed CRM, membership and member-portal scope, the fee is £2,500 for configuration, the agreed data import and training, followed by £4,800 per year for hosted use, updates and business-hours support. This excludes VAT where applicable, provider charges and bespoke integrations. We begin with a £500 evaluation, credited against onboarding if you proceed.
